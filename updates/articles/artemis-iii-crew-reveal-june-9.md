NASA will name the four Artemis III astronauts on Tuesday, June 9 at 11:00 a.m. EDT (17:00 CEST), at the Johnson Space Center in Houston. The event streams on NASA+ and the agency's YouTube channel. Live coverage will run roughly one hour, and the crew names will appear in Artemis Tracker the moment they are read out.

The four names will be assigned to a mission that was substantially rewritten earlier this year. In February 2026, NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman confirmed that Artemis III will fly as a low Earth orbit test flight in mid-2027, with the first crewed lunar landing deferred to Artemis IV in 2028. The agency's May 26 media advisory describes the mission as one that "will test critical rendezvous and docking capabilities between Orion and commercial human landing systems," with the crew spending more time aboard Orion than on Artemis II and demonstrating docking system performance for the first time.

That is the mission these four people are training for. No lunar surface. No descent. The Apollo-style boots-on-the-Moon imagery that has anchored the program publicly belongs to Artemis IV now.

## What the mission looks like

The current concept calls for an SLS launch from Kennedy Space Center, four astronauts in Orion, and rendezvous and docking with at least one commercial Human Landing System in low Earth orbit at roughly 463 km altitude. SpaceX's Starship HLS and Blue Origin's Blue Moon Mark 2 are both notionally in scope. NASA has not committed publicly to whether both will participate, only one, or whether the demonstration will be staged across more than one flight.

The crew is also expected to enter at least one of the lander test articles to evaluate internal volume, life support, and ergonomics, and to validate the new Axiom and Prada-built lunar spacesuits in orbit before any surface use. Mission duration is expected to exceed Artemis II's roughly 10-day flight.

## Why now

A crew reveal one year before launch is on the early end for NASA, but not unprecedented. The practical reason to name four specific people now is hardware fit: cabin interfaces, suit sizing, docking ergonomics, and seat geometry on both the SpaceX and Blue Origin landers cannot be finalised against generic crew assumptions. Locking in four bodies — with measured heights, reach, and hand size — gives both contractors a fixed target.

## The Blue Origin question

The reveal lands less than two weeks after a significant setback for one of the two HLS partners. On the evening of May 28, 2026, a Blue Origin New Glenn rocket exploded during a static fire test at Launch Complex 36 at Cape Canaveral. The vehicle was being prepared for an Amazon Leo satellite launch, but New Glenn is also the launcher Blue Origin intends to use for Blue Moon. Isaacman issued a statement the same evening: "Spaceflight is unforgiving, and developing new heavy-lift launch capability is extraordinarily difficult. We will work with our partners to support a thorough investigation of this anomaly, assess near-term mission impacts, and get back to launching rockets."

Blue Origin's leadership has since said it expects New Glenn to fly again before the end of 2026. Whether a human-rated Blue Moon Mk.2 article can be ready for an Artemis III docking demonstration in mid-2027 is now a live question NASA has not yet answered. The most plausible fallback — an Artemis III that flies with SpaceX's Starship HLS only — would not change the crew assignments. It would change what they get to dock with.

## What to watch on June 9

Three things matter beyond the names. First, whether NASA reaffirms the mid-2027 launch target or hedges it. Second, whether the agency commits to which lander or landers will participate in the docking demonstration. Third, any update on the long-pole hardware milestones: Starship's orbital propellant transfer demonstration, New Glenn's return to flight, and the SLS core stage for Artemis III, whose RS-25 engines are scheduled to arrive at Kennedy in July.

The names will be the headline. The hardware is what will determine whether the mid-2027 date holds.
