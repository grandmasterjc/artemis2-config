The Artemis program didn't end with Artemis II — it's just getting started. Here's where NASA is heading over the next decade.

## Artemis III (~2027)

A crewed orbital test. Astronauts will launch on SLS/Orion, dock with SpaceX Starship HLS in low Earth orbit, and validate the systems needed for lunar landing.

## Artemis IV (~2028)

The first crewed lunar landing since 1972. Two astronauts will descend to the south pole via Starship HLS.

## Artemis V (~2029)

Expanded lunar surface operations with Blue Origin's Blue Moon Mk2 lander. Begins construction of long-term surface infrastructure.

## Beyond

NASA's goal is sustained lunar presence by the early 2030s — and Mars eventually.
