## A New Kind of Lander

Starship HLS is a variant of SpaceX's fully reusable Starship vehicle, modified for lunar operations. At roughly 50 meters tall, it dwarfs the Apollo Lunar Module — and is designed to carry far more crew and cargo to the surface.

## Key Specifications

- **Height:** ~50 meters
- **Propellant:** Liquid oxygen and liquid methane
- **Crew capacity:** Up to 4 astronauts
- **Payload to lunar surface:** 100+ tonnes

## How Artemis III Works

Unlike Apollo, which launched everything on a single rocket, Artemis III uses a distributed architecture:

1. SpaceX launches Starship HLS to a staging orbit
2. Multiple tanker flights refuel it with propellant
3. NASA launches the crew on SLS/Orion
4. Orion docks with Starship HLS in lunar orbit
5. The crew transfers and descends to the surface
