Timeline

Artemis II mission phases
LaunchT+0COMPLETE
SLS Block 1 lifts off from Launch Complex 39B at Kennedy Space Center carrying Orion and its four-person crew.
AscentT+8mCOMPLETE
Solid boosters separate, core stage cutoff, and the ICPS places Orion in a high Earth orbit for systems checkout.
TLI BurnT+2hCOMPLETE
The trans-lunar injection burn sends Orion out of Earth orbit onto a free-return trajectory toward the Moon.
OutboundT+1dCOMPLETE
Orion coasts outbound for roughly four days. The crew checks life support, navigation, and manual handling.
Lunar FlybyT+4d 12hCOMPLETE
Orion sweeps around the far side of the Moon — the farthest humans have traveled from Earth.
Return TransitT+6dCOMPLETE
On the free-return trajectory, Orion falls back toward Earth over roughly four days.
EntryT+8d 22hCOMPLETE
Orion separates from its service module and enters the atmosphere at ~40,000 km/h behind its heat shield.
SplashdownT+9dCOMPLETE
Orion splashes down in the Pacific Ocean off San Diego. USS San Diego and recovery teams retrieve the crew. Mission elapsed time: ~9 days.
